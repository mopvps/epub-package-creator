Preface to the Second Edition

The first edition of this book, Civil engineering insurance and bonding, was published in 1987. It used the insurance clauses of the then current ICE Conditions of Contract for Works of Civil Engineering Construction, 5th Edition, as a basis for describing various aspects of civil engineering insurance. Since then the ICE 5th Edition has been superseded by the ICE 6th Edition and several new ICE conditions of contract (as well as revisions to them) have also been issued. It was felt by the Editorial Panel of the ICE Engineering Management Series of books (of which this book was the first) that the time had come for an update.

The second edition follows the same format as the first edition but now covers, in particular, the following conditions of contract:

  • ICE Works of Civil Engineering Construction, 6th Edition (1991)
  • ICE Design and Construct (1992)
  • ICE Minor Works, 2nd Edition (1995)
  • ICE NEC Engineering and Construction Contract, 2nd Edition (1995)

Much use has been made of Peter Madge’s original text and all he said in his Preface to the First Edition still holds good. Besides the obvious changes necessitated by the use of clauses from the above conditions of contract instead of ICE 5th Edition, the opportunity has been taken to update the text where considered appropriate. This includes a complete rewrite of the chapter dealing with the significance of insurance in risk management.

We are greatly indebted to the groundwork so expertly laid by Peter Madge in the text of the original book and to the advice and encouragement he has provided following his review of our initial draft.

Leslie Edwards

Geoffrey Lord

London, February 1996
